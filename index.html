Our sustainable impact

Turning waste into cleaner energy.

Our sustainability initiatives combine biomass fuel, recycled-waste steam generation and solar power to reduce dependence on non-renewable resources.

4,500Tons of bio-fuel generated per month
70%Fuel cost reduction
0%NOx & SOx emission
20 kWSolar-based power generation

Pakistan annually produces about 50 million tons of agricultural waste, a potential resource for producing biofuels or biogas. Manufacturing biomass pellets from agricultural waste supports the transition to renewable energy, fosters environmentally friendly practices and reduces reliance on non-renewable resources.

Supplementing our biomass efforts, efficient processes generate steam from recycled waste. This eco-friendly alternative to traditional coal-based methods enhances energy efficiency and aligns with our commitment to sustainable industrial practices.

A 20 kW solar power system at our facilities supports our commitment to diversified energy sources, reducing our carbon footprint and protecting our planet.
