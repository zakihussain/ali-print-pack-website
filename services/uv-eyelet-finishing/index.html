UV & eyelet finishing

Protective coating and display-ready reinforcement for retail-facing print components.

This section combines UV coating and eyelet finishing to support stronger surface performance and functional hanging presentation. It is suited to tags, cards and display-oriented products that require added durability or attachment hardware.

Surface enhancement

UV finishing helps improve gloss, handling resistance and the visual finish of selected printed work.

Functional reinforcement

Eyelet installation supports hanging applications and product attachment where physical hardware is required.

Retail presentation

The combined process supports items that need both stronger appearance and practical end-use preparation.
