Computer to plate (CTP).

Our in-house CTP facility supports film-free plate preparation, stronger prepress accuracy and controlled output for reliable printing and packaging production.

CTP

Direct digital plate preparation with stronger prepress control.

Computer-to-plate converts approved digital artwork into offset printing plates. This removes film negatives and supports cleaner prepress accuracy, consistency and control before printing starts.

Film-free preparation

Our in-house setup removes film negatives and keeps plate preparation fully digital.

Sharp detail and consistency

Plate imaging is managed for stronger detail reproduction and more stable print preparation.

Standards and specifications

Production is aligned with industry standards and client specifications for required quality output.

Three-step CTP preparation.

Each stage is managed to maintain accuracy, plate quality and production readiness.

01

Artwork and design approval

Approved design files are finalized for accurate prepress preparation.

02

Plate imaging and exposure

Digital data is transferred to the plate with controlled imaging and exposure.

03

Processing and press readiness

The imaged plate is processed and prepared for stable production use.
